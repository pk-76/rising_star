ABOUTWESTERN AT RISING STAR

WHY Western?

Western dance brings together hip-hop, commercial and freestyle styles. Our classes in Guwahati keep things energetic and approachable — perfect for teens, beginners and anyone who wants to move to today’s music.

Our mentors teach with patience and energy, in a friendly, safe and supportive studio — and every dancer gets a chance to perform. Your first class is free, so you can feel the floor before you commit.

WHAT YOU’LL LEARN

  • Hip-hop foundations and grooves
  • Commercial choreography
  • Freestyle and cypher basics
  • Music-video style routines

Yes. Your first Western class at Rising Star is free. Book it online in under a minute and we’ll confirm the best batch on WhatsApp.

Absolutely. We start from step one, so beginners of every age are welcome. Many of our students had never danced before they joined.
